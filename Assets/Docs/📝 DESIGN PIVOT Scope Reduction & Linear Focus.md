The Core Realization

The scope of an endless, procedurally generated campaign is overwhelming and actively preventing progress. The primary goal has always been tense, highly-controlled tactical combat (XCOM's combat stakes), not managing a massive strategic spreadsheet.

By stripping away the complex meta-layer and procedural generation, we can hand-craft a tighter, more meaningful experience. The strategy layer only exists to give the tactical layer stakes—and we can achieve those stakes with a much smaller footprint.

The New Campaign Structure

 Linear Campaign: Exactly 5 hand-crafted missions played in sequence.

 No Procedural Generation: Hand-designed maps and enemy placements. This makes it much easier to execute the "Cop Vision" (e.g., tightly scripting a hostage standoff or pacing enemy aggression) without fighting a random generator.

 Win State: Beat all 5 missions. The final score is based on tactical performance and how many troops survived.

The Roster & Tactical Stakes

 Fixed Starting Roster: Start the campaign with exactly 5 troops.

 Deployment: Bring only 3 or 4 troops into a given mission.

 The Consequence Loop:

 If 1 troop dies: It hurts emotionally and limits your bench, but the campaign is still winnable.

 If 2 troops die: You are now at a severe tactical disadvantage for the late-game 4-man missions.

 Why this works: It creates immediate, tangible tension without needing complex recruitment/replacement systems.

The Stripped-Down Meta-Game

 Between Missions: A simple Shop Interface.

 Economy: You are awarded money based on your tactical scoring (hostages saved, no civilians harmed, etc.). Use that money to buy gear/items for the next linear level.

 Cut Features (For Now): Precinct upgrades, automated daily recruit intake, dynamic map nodes, complex retirement percentages.

Future Expansion (The "Open Door")

This is a complete, shippable slice of the game. Building it this way does not actively prevent adding procedural elements later. Once these 5 levels are fully playable and fun, a random mission generator, dynamic campaign map, and deeper roster systems can be built on top of this solid foundation. Finish the core first.
